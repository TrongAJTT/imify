🎉 Welcome to Imify v2.3.3!

This update brings **Continuous Multi-Section Collage Browsing** with quick-jump navigation, interactive **Direct Canvas Row & Column Reordering** in Grid Designer, **Collapsible Accordion Cards** in Asset Management, **Multiline Captions** in Splicing, and refined **Notification & Dialog Experiences** across all tools.

✨ Major Highlights (v2.3):

- 🖼️ **Collage Maker & Layout Presets**: Continuous layout browsing from 2 to 10 photos, sticky headers, quick-jump navigation, drag-and-drop reordering, and direct clipboard image intake.
- 📐 **Interactive Grid Designer**: Direct canvas drag-and-drop row/column reordering with spatial drop snapping, primary direction toggle, and one-click Collage Preset saving.
- 📄 **PDF Studio Suite**: 2-way PDF conversion with password-protected PDF unlocking, high-DPI rasterization, and ZIP archiving.
- ✍️ **Text Layers & Typography**: Full text layer editing with native editable PSD export, gradient fills, shadows, and snap guides in Image Filling.
- 💬 **Splicing Caption System**: Inside/outside multiline captions with 9-point visual picker, rounded corners, and Ctrl+Enter confirmation.
- 🔍 **Unified Canvas Viewport**: Smooth pan/zoom navigation, 65% screen height viewport, and touch-optimized selection priority.
- 🛠️ **Emergency Recovery Mode**: Dedicated `/recovery` page to diagnose cache anomalies and restore app access safely.
